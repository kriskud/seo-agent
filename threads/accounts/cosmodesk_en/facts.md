The only source of truth about the product. Anything not listed here must not be mentioned.

## In one line
CosmoDesk is scheduling and client management for independent beauty professionals: appointments, client records, online booking and supply tracking in one workspace.

## What it does
- Appointments and calendar: the whole day at a glance — who is coming, which treatment, how long it takes.
- Online booking: the professional shares a link; the client picks a service, date and an available time; the booking appears in the calendar. Works 24/7. No separate website needed.
- Client records: visit history, notes, before/after photos next to the visit date and treatment.
- Allergies and contraindications are visible right in the appointment.
- Product and supply tracking: usage recorded per treatment, a heads-up when stock is running low.
- Return visits: shows who hasn't visited recently.
- Finances: revenue, average visit value, client numbers, monthly trends.
- Working hours and available time slots.
- Telegram notifications about bookings.
- Works on a phone.
- Built for solo practitioners, no receptionist needed.

## Pricing and trial
- 14-day free trial, no card required.
- All plans include the same features: $14.90 billed monthly; $39.90 every 3 months; $119.90 per year.
